Ludo

🔗 Two tokens are on the same square.
Make a pair? (moves together, half the roll, safe from single kicks)
Loading match...

Your own colour's corner is always at the bottom-left of your screen. Raw tokens wait in their corner until you roll a 6, then they come out onto your start square (a star). Stars are safe squares. A six gives another roll. Your rolls are saved, and after your last roll they are used in the order you rolled them (tap a roll chip if you want to use one first): tap the token to move. If only one token can move, it moves by itself. Put two of your tokens (or a team mate's) on one square and tap Pair: they move together by half of each roll (4 = 2 squares, odd rolls can't move a pair), a single token cannot kick them (only another pair can), and they split only when they reach a safe square. Three sixes in a row cancel all your rolls. A kick or reaching Home gives a bonus roll, but the token that kicked must stay put. No time limit. A single token cannot jump over an opponent's pair: it must land exactly on the pair's square first.

🃏 Twenty-Nine

How 29 works
  1. 4 players. You and the player opposite you are a team.
  2. Each player gets 4 cards first. Bid from 16 to 29 for how many points your team will win. Passing is final.
  3. The highest bidder puts one card face down. Its suit is the secret trump suit. Then everyone gets 4 more cards.
  4. Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you cannot, you may press "Show trump". The trump suit is revealed and you must play a trump card if you have one. Or just play any card without asking.
  5. Once trump is shown, a trump card beats the other suits. Before that, only the led suit can win.
  6. Card order: J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7. Points: J = 3, 9 = 2, A = 1, 10 = 1. The last trick is worth 1 more. In total 29.
  7. Marriage: after trump is shown, if your team has won a trick and you hold the King and Queen of trumps, declare it on your turn. The bidding team's target drops by 4. If the other team declares it, the target goes up by 4.
  8. The bidding team wins +1 game point if it reaches its target, otherwise -1. First team to +6 wins. A team that falls to -6 loses.

🏙️ Monopoly

How Monopoly works (simplified)
  1. Roll the dice and move around the board. Everyone starts with cash and gets 200 for passing START.
  2. Land on a free place and you may buy it. Land on someone else's place and you pay rent.
  3. Own every place of one colour to double the rent and to build houses (build evenly, up to a hotel).
  4. Doubles let you roll again. Three doubles in a row send you to Jail. In Jail pay 50, roll a double, or wait 3 turns.
  5. Short of cash? Buildings are sold and places are mortgaged automatically. If that is not enough you are bankrupt.
  6. Classic: the last player left wins. Quick: after 20 rounds the player with the highest net worth wins.
  7. There are no auctions or trades in this version.
